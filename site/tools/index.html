Autonomous Driving: AI Safety and Security

Interactive safety tools

Small calculators that accompany the course notebooks. They run entirely in your browser, work on a phone, and use the same tables and formulas as the tested Python package avsafety, which is checked against them in CI.

ASIL calculator

Severity, exposure and controllability → ASIL, with the Table 4 slice and decomposition options. ISO 26262-3.

TARA risk calculator

Impact and attack feasibility (attack potential, attack vector or CVSS v3.1) → risk value and CAL. ISO/SAE 21434.

RSS safe distance

Responsibility-Sensitive Safety minimum gap with TTC, headway and DRAC, and the safety–usefulness trade-off.

How many miles?

Failure-free distance needed to demonstrate a target rate with a given confidence, and the fleet-years it takes.

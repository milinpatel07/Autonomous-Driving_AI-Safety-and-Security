Pick the route that matches your goal. Each path is an order of notebooks, not a schedule: go as deep as your work needs.

::::{grid} 1 1 2 2

:::{card} Complete course
All eight modules in order: 01 → 08. Recommended for students and for engineers new to AV safety.
:::

:::{card} Safety engineer
03 Functional Safety → 04 SOTIF → 05 Cybersecurity → 08 Standards, Regulation and Safety Case.
Focus: work products you will produce in industry (HARA, FMEDA, FTA, SOTIF analysis, TARA, safety case).
:::

:::{card} ML / perception engineer
01 Perception → 02 Failures and Robustness → 06 AI Safety → 04-03 OOD monitoring → 07-01 Runtime monitoring.
Focus: why models fail, how to measure and bound uncertainty, and what evidence a safety case needs from ML.
:::

:::{card} Security engineer
05 Cybersecurity (all) → 02-04 Adversarial attacks → 07-02 V2X → 08-01 Standards integration.
Focus: TARA, in-vehicle networks, physical-world attacks on perception, safety–security co-engineering.
:::

:::{card} Manager / regulator
01-01 SAE levels and ODD → 02-01 Incident case studies → 03-01, 04-01, 05-01, 06-01 (the four standards in brief) → 08-02 Regulation → 08-04 Safety case.
:::

:::{card} One-hour taster
01-01 SAE levels and ODD → 03-02 HARA → the [interactive tools](interactive-tools.md).
:::
::::

## Prerequisites

- Python basics (NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib). Every notebook explains its code.
- No GPU, no dataset download, no local install needed: use the **Open in Colab** badge at the top of each notebook.
- Standards are paywalled; the notebooks explain their concepts and cite clause numbers so that you can follow up with the official texts.
